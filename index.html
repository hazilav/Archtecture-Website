METHODOLOGY

The 5-Step Architectural Timeline.

From initial CAD vector ingestion to final 8K color-graded films, our structured workflow ensures absolute precision at every milestone.

01
STAGE 01

CAD / BIM Data Ingestion

We receive Revit, ArchiCAD, Rhino, or AutoCAD files. We audit geometry, sanitize polygon layers, and align coordinates for 3D staging.

02
STAGE 02

3D Geometry Modeling

Refining architectural details, constructing site topographies, modeling custom millwork, and populating 3D foliage and surroundings.

03
STAGE 03

Material Texturing & Lighting

Applying PBR physically-based shaders (brushed metal, marble, oak) and setting solar sun vectors & volumetric fog lighting.

04
STAGE 04

Cinematic Camera Choreography

Directing camera movement paths, focal lengths, depth-of-field racks, and pacing to craft an emotional architectural narrative.

05
STAGE 05

8K Raytraced Render & Post

Distributing frames across high-performance render farms followed by DaVinci Resolve color grading, motion blur, and spatial audio mastering.
